# Parasitic Artificial Organism: Short-Form Video

**Abstract**

The paper conceptualizes short-form videos (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels) as symbiotic parasitic artificial organisms that live only when they occupy a viewer's attention, and otherwise remain dormant. It examines how these videos exhibit life-like characteristics — inert in isolation but capable of infecting audiences and spreading through cognitive and social networks. The analysis draws on film theory, posthumanist philosophy, and empirical research, situating the argument within theoretical frameworks from Deleuze, Michel Serres, and Nick Land while investigating the cognitive and neuropsychological effects of short-form media consumption.
