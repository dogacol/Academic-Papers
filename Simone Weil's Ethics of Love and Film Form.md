# Simone Weil's Ethics of Love and Film Form

**Abstract**

The article argues that Simone Weil's thought provides meaningful insights into filmmaking and ethics. Through three key concepts — attention, *metaxu*, and decreation — the paper contends that film is structurally suited to embody what these concepts mandate. The collaborative nature of filmmaking and its technical mediation through cameras and editing align with Weil's ethical philosophy. The paper concludes that both filmmaking and watching films can be understood as practices of love when examined through a Weilian lens.

**Published in:** *Studies in Christian Ethics* (SAGE), OnlineFirst (2026). DOI: [10.1177/09539468261436313](https://doi.org/10.1177/09539468261436313)
