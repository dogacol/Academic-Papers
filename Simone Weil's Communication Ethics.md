# Simone Weil's Communication Ethics

**Abstract**

The article explores how Simone Weil conceptualizes love as radical attention that suspends egoistic concerns to pursue solely the good of another. It argues that her concepts of attention, *décréation*, and *metaxu* address a gap in contemporary communication ethics by offering frameworks for overcoming self-interest and recognizing others' needs transparently. The paper grounds Weil's ethics in Platonic thought and mysticism, placing her ideas in dialogue with current communication ethics and demonstrating their practical relevance.
