# Ethical and Safety Issues in Digital Media Literacy: Media Awareness

**Abstract**

The study examines ethical and security concerns in digital media literacy among Turkish university students using a quantitative survey approach. With 704 participants, the research found that despite frequent digital platform use, students lack sufficient knowledge about algorithmic operations, ethical implications, and security risks. The findings reveal positive correlations between content filtering, automated decision-making, and ethical awareness, with older students and those with longer digital usage demonstrating higher algorithmic literacy levels. However, reliance on social media for information verification increases exposure to misinformation. The authors recommend integrating digital literacy education into curricula and fostering critical media consumption practices.
